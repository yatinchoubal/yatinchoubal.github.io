I'm a **Director of Product Management at a leading global video game company**, focused on game developer experience. Before that, I worked in product at a global digital payments company, a global technology company, and a global tax services firm.

Much of my job is translation: taking deep technical work and explaining it to senior leaders as business outcomes. Resumes, interviews, and promotion cases test the same skill. Can you explain what you did and why it mattered? Many capable people can't yet, and that's where I help.

*This is an independent practice I run personally. It isn't affiliated with any current or former employer.*
