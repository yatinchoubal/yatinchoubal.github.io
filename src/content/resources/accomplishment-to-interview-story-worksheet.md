Most people have plenty of strong material. The trouble is it lives in their heads as "stuff I worked on," not as stories with a situation, a decision, and a result. This worksheet walks you through one accomplishment at a time:

- **Capture the facts** while you still remember them: context, your role, constraints, and what changed.
- **Find honest evidence**, whether that's a metric, a reliable estimate (clearly labeled), or a specific qualitative outcome.
- **Shape the story** into a 60-to-90-second answer with a clear beginning, middle, and end.
- **Reuse it** as a resume bullet and a line in your next self-review.

It includes prompts to check that every claim is true and that you can explain it under follow-up questions.
