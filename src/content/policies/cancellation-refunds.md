I want the rules to be clear before you pay. This policy applies together with the [Service Terms](/policies/service-terms/).

## Rescheduling

- Reschedule up to **{{rescheduleNoticeHours}} hours** before a session at no cost. Reply to your confirmation email or use its link.
- Sessions rescheduled with less notice, or missed without notice, count as used. I may make an exception for genuine emergencies.
- Rescheduled sessions still need to happen within your service's time limit.

## Cancellations and refunds

| Situation | Refund |
|---|---|
| Single session or review, canceled at least {{rescheduleNoticeHours}} hours before the session (or before I start the review) | Full refund |
| Single session canceled with less notice, or missed | No refund |
| Written review (such as the annotated feedback in the Resume & LinkedIn Review) after I've started it | No refund |
| Package canceled within {{refundRequestDays}} days of purchase, before the first session | Full refund |
| Package canceled at any other time | Refund for unused sessions and undelivered documents, at the per-session value of the package |
| Group clinic canceled at least {{rescheduleNoticeHours}} hours before the start time | Full refund, or a transfer to another clinic date |
| Digital products | No refund once downloaded, unless the file is defective |
| I cancel and can't offer a suitable new time | Full refund for the affected portion |
| I decline or end an engagement because of a conflict of interest | Full refund for anything not yet delivered |

**Per-session value** means the package price divided by the number of sessions and written deliverables it includes, as listed on the service page.

Refunds aren't available because a hiring or promotion outcome didn't happen. Coaching doesn't guarantee outcomes.

## How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are sent within 5 business days.

- **PayPal and Venmo:** refunded through the same platform to the account that paid. The provider decides when the money reaches you.
- **Zelle:** Zelle payments can't be reversed, so I send the refund as a new Zelle payment to the same email or phone number that paid.

I won't send a refund to a different person or account.

## Installments

- Installment plans are a commitment to the full package price. They aren't a cancel-anytime subscription.
- I email a payment request before each installment is due. Nothing is charged automatically, and no interest, finance charges, or late fees are added.
- If you cancel a package on installments, the refund rules above apply to the total package price. If you've paid more than the value of the work delivered, I refund the difference. If you've paid less, the difference for completed work is still due. Future installments are canceled.
- If an installment isn't paid within 7 days of its due date, I may pause remaining sessions and deliverables until it's paid.

## Unpaid order requests

Order requests expire after **{{manualPaymentHoldHours}} hours** if I haven't verified the payment. Nothing is owed for an expired request. A booking isn't confirmed until I've verified the payment in my receiving account.

## How to request a cancellation or refund

Use the [contact form](/contact/) and include your order reference. I'll reply within {{messagingResponseBusinessDays}} business days.
