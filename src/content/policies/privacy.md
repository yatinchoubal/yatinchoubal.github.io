This policy explains how Yatin Choubal ("I", "me") handles personal information collected through this website and while delivering coaching services. I collect only the information I need to deliver the services you request. I don't sell or share your personal information for advertising.

## What I collect

| Information | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Name and email | Booking, contact form, newsletter signup | To deliver services, reply to you, and send emails you asked for |
| Target role, experience level, goals, time zone | Booking | To prepare for your service |
| Resume, job description, or other documents (optional) | Through the upload link after your payment is confirmed | To deliver reviews and coaching |
| Order reference, amount, and payment status | Booking and payment | To confirm, account for, and refund your purchase |
| Name and email you use on the booking calendar | Booking a session time | To schedule the session and send the meeting link |
| Basic, aggregated site analytics | Visiting the site | To understand which pages are useful |

I don't collect or store card numbers, bank details, or payment logins. You pay on PayPal's, Venmo's, or your bank's own app or website. I see only what those providers show the recipient, such as your name, the amount, and the note you add.

## Documents

- Documents are never published on this website or made searchable.
- Please don't upload confidential employer information.
- Documents are deleted **{{documentRetentionDays}} days** after your service is complete, unless you ask me to delete them sooner or we agree to keep them longer for an active package.
- I don't put your documents into AI tools without your permission.

## Payment privacy

- **Venmo** payments can be visible in Venmo's public feed unless you set the payment to Private. I recommend Private, and asking for only your order reference in the note.
- Payment providers keep their own records of your payment under their own privacy policies.
- I keep payment records for as long as tax and accounting rules require.

## Email and marketing

- Booking a service or contacting me **doesn't** add you to my newsletter.
- The newsletter is opt-in only. Every newsletter email includes an unsubscribe link.
- Booking and service emails (confirmations, payment requests, reminders, receipts) are sent because they're needed to deliver your service.

## Service providers

I use these providers to run this site and deliver services. They process your information on my behalf or as independent services, under their own privacy policies:

- **Netlify:** website hosting, and the booking and contact forms
- **Google:** Google Calendar (session booking), Google Meet (video sessions), Google Drive (document upload and storage), and Gmail (email)
- **PayPal and Venmo:** payments, if you choose them
- **Your bank and Zelle:** payments, if you choose Zelle
- **Plausible Analytics:** privacy-friendly site statistics, if enabled
- **Newsletter provider:** only if you sign up

## Analytics

This site uses privacy-friendly analytics that don't use cookies or track you across other websites. I see aggregated figures such as page views and button clicks.

## Your choices and rights

You can ask me to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to delete your documents early. Use the [contact form](/contact/) and choose "Privacy or document deletion request." I'll confirm within {{messagingResponseBusinessDays}} business days. Some records, such as payment records, may need to be kept for legal or tax reasons. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law, and I'll honor them.

## Security

I use reputable providers, keep access to client information limited to me, and use strong account security. No method of storing or sending information is completely secure, so please share only what's needed for your service.

## Children

These services are for adults. I don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

## Where information is processed

I'm based in the United States, and my providers store information in the United States and other countries. By using this site, you understand your information will be processed there.
