I want this website and my services to be usable by everyone.

## The website

This site is designed with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA, as the target. It hasn't been formally audited, and some parts may fall short. It includes:

- Keyboard navigation for all menus, forms, and controls, with visible focus indicators
- A "skip to main content" link
- Labeled form fields and clear error messages
- Text and background colors chosen for readable contrast
- Respect for your device's reduced-motion setting
- Descriptive text alternatives for meaningful images

Some third-party tools, such as Google Calendar booking, Google Drive, PayPal, and Venmo, are provided by other companies. I choose providers with accessibility in mind, but I don't control their interfaces.

## Sessions and materials

If you need an accommodation, just ask. For example: captions during video sessions, extra time, materials in a particular format, or a different way to share documents. You don't need to explain why.
